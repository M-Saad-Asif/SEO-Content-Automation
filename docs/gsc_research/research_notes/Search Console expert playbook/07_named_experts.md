Research date: 2026-11-01. Sources were found by web search and fetch. LinkedIn, X and Reddit pages often blocked full fetches (primaryposition.com returned HTTP 403, and Growth Memo is paywalled), so some items rely on search-result snippets. Those items are marked **(snippet)**. Nothing below is a verbatim quote unless it is a regex pattern or a GSC prompt string.

## Q1. Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR (Holistic SEO): GSC-linked tactics

### Takeaway
Koray uses Search Console data as the scoreboard for his Topical Authority framework. He tracks daily impressions as the main early KPI (re-ranking begins at about 10,000+ daily impressions), counts ranking queries as a measure of how well the topical map is covered, and reads branded and non-branded impressions moving together as evidence of growing authority. He pairs this with crawl-efficiency ("cost of retrieval") KPIs. His public material is mostly self-reported case studies, and I found no GSC click-by-click tutorial from him.

### Cited Findings
- **T1 – Impressions-first KPI for new sites / re-ranking threshold.** Trigger: a new project. Look at daily impressions rather than clicks. Rule: once a site passes **10,000+ daily impressions**, he says re-ranking begins and "positive ranking states" appear. Action at that point: scale topical coverage quickly, because momentum (the speed of publishing and updating) multiplies impact. Interview dated **2025-11-05** — [SEO by Marta interview](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
- **T2 – Initial ranking vs re-ranking.** He describes initial ranking as a temporary test of early user and system reactions. Re-ranking is a deeper evaluation that uses historical data to decide whether a source deserves higher positions. GSC implication: early impression and position swings on new content are test noise, and the durable signal is the re-ranking phase. — [SEO by Marta, 2025-11-05](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
- **T3 – Momentum / positive ranking state.** Once a site enters a positive ranking state, it keeps rising despite short-term mistakes until the state changes. Tactic: build one strong subfolder into a positive state, then apply that subfolder's "character" to other sections. In GSC terms, watch performance filtered by subfolder (page filter) and expand from the winning subfolder. — [SEO by Marta, 2025-11-05](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
- **T4 – Refresh cadence.** Update every document at least once every 6 months. He also says uncontrolled aggressive publishing triggered manual penalties after 2021, so momentum has to be "controlled, layered, contextually consistent". — [SEO by Marta](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
- **T5 – Core-update timing.** He predicts the next broad core update from past cycles and ships major changes **15–20 days before** it. He treats winning several consecutive core updates (he cites March and June 2025) as the sign that topical authority is being established. GSC use: annotate update dates and compare periods across each update. — [SEO by Marta](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
- **T6 – Branded demand as the critical KPI, plus brand/non-brand co-movement.** He calls branded search demand the most critical pattern. In an X case study (SaaS recovery), he attributed the recovery to three drivers: (1) cost of retrieval (URL count cut sharply, fewer non-canonical URLs), (2) non-branded impressions rising in step with branded search demand, and (3) new content on the outer layers of the topical map. He cautioned that the project was only in its third month. — [X post (snippet)](https://x.com/KorayGubur/status/1965191271330259231); [SEO by Marta](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
- **T7 – Cost-of-retrieval technical KPIs.** Targets: server response under 100 ms, 99% of HTML crawl hits on canonical URLs, and 99% 200/304 status codes. These map onto the GSC Crawl Stats report (by response, by file type, by purpose). — [SEO by Marta](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/). Caveat: Google says Crawl Stats counts the actual URLs requested and does not assign them to canonicals, and it counts each redirect hop separately — [Google Crawl Stats help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9679690?hl=en)
- **T8 – Query-count growth as the topical-map coverage metric.** His case studies report success as growth in total ranking queries from GSC, for example "3,600-query increase in total ranking queries" and 1,200%+ clicks after expanding a topical map — [Holistic SEO topical map case study](https://www.holisticseo.digital/seo-research-study/topical-map). Another study reports "36,000+ new organic search queries" and +115% clicks in 3 months YoY — [Holistic SEO case study](https://www.holisticseo.digital/seo-research-study/holistic-seo). A further study reports a +98% organic query count — [Lexical semantics study](https://www.holisticseo.digital/seo-research-study/lexical-semantics). These pages are several years old (pre-2023).
- **T9 – Migration YoY comparison.** He reported a WordPress→Next.js migration as last 3 months vs the same period last year: clicks 4.44M→8.16M, impressions 52.4M→81M, CTR 8.5%→10.1%, average position 7.7→5. He attributed the jump to the migration. — [X post (snippet)](https://x.com/KorayGubur/status/2112649152219791410)
- **Formula (conceptual, not a GSC metric):** ((Historical Data × Topical Coverage) ÷ Cost of Retrieval) × Right Visual Annotations — Search Engine Land article (via [X announcement, snippet](https://x.com/KorayGubur/status/2077139044904108372?lang=en))
- **Third-party operationalisation (not Koray's own words):** Export 90 days of queries, strip brand terms, group the queries into repeated "query templates", and track impressions and clicks per template. — [SEOteric](https://www.seoteric.com/understanding-query-templates-and-topical-authority-in-seo/)

### Inferences
- Automation mapping: (a) a daily-impressions trend with a 10k/day milestone flag; (b) a weekly count of distinct queries, overall and per topical-map cluster (page-path or regex filter); (c) branded vs non-branded impression ratio over time; (d) Crawl Stats share of 200/304 responses and average response time against his 99% and 100 ms targets; (e) a stale-content list of URLs not updated in more than 6 months.
- His T8 "query count" is easy to compute via the API (distinct queries per page or folder per period). It is affected by GSC's anonymised-query filtering (see Q6).

### Gaps
- I found no Koray video transcript that shows his actual GSC screen workflow (filters, regex). His YouTube case studies were not fetchable as transcripts within budget.
- "Using GSC to measure query-network growth" is inferred from his reported metrics. I found no step-by-step from him.

## Q2. Edward Sturm: GSC tactics

### Takeaway
Sturm teaches simple, repeatable GSC loops. He finds queries at position 3–20 with impressions, then either expands the ranking page or creates a dedicated page. He does query-cluster expansion of top pages using an exported query CSV plus Claude. He uses GSC's AI-filter prompts for funnel and intent segments, and he requests indexing after every edit.

### Cited Findings
- **S1 – Setup for new sites.** Connect a Domain Property, submit sitemaps, request indexing for key pages, then review what you rank for. — [Sturm, "GSC's New AI Update…", 2026-03-09](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-search-console-ai-seo-wins/). After adding or editing any page, submit its URL in the top "Inspect any URL" bar. Technical SEO beyond this setup only matters for non-standard CMSs, huge sites, programmatic SEO, custom web apps, or migrations. — [Sturm, "Pareto SEO", 2026-03-13](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/)
- **S2 – Low-hanging-fruit keywords (positions 3–20).** Performance > Search results > export (CSV/Sheets/Excel) > filter to positions **3–20**. Then turn on Average position, click the query, and see which page ranks. Decision rule: if the query is relevant to that page, add more text about it to the relevant section (if the exact keyword is missing, add it plus a few sentences). If it is not relevant enough, create a new dedicated page and link to it from the currently ranking page. — [Pareto SEO, 2026-03-13](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/)
- **S3 – AI-filter intent prompts (GSC's natural-language filter).** Performance > the blue AI filter button. Prompts he uses verbatim: "Show me bottom of funnel searches"; "Show me queries with local intent like near me, nearby, closest, open now, local services, etc."; "Show queries where users are searching for a service provider, agency, consultant, or company related to the services my site offers"; "Show me my branded searches"; "Show me comparison and evaluation queries including vs, versus, alternative, comparison, review, best"; "Which queries had the biggest increase in impressions over the last 3 months compared to the previous period?" Then turn on Avg position and CTR, Filter rows Position > **3**, sort by position, and set rows to 500. Keep queries with high impressions, position **3–20**, low CTR, and strong business relevance. Action: on the top-ranking page for the query, add the term near the top, add a section, or update the H1, an H2 or the title. If no page fits, create one with the term at the start of the title, in the H1, the first sentence, the slug and the meta description. — [Sturm, 2026-03-09](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-search-console-ai-seo-wins/)
- **S4 – Monitoring prompts and folder structure.** "What is my /services/ pages' CTR last week compared to the previous period?"; "What are my /blog/ clicks and average position compared to last month?"; "Which pages had the biggest drop in clicks compared to last month?" Put service pages in subfolders such as /services/ so they are easy to segment. — [Sturm, 2026-03-09](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-search-console-ai-seo-wins/)
- **S5 – "1-hour update" for best pages plus internal links to weak pages.** (1) Performance > Pages, choose a top page, open its queries, and Export CSV. (2) Give Claude the page content and the query CSV, and ask for keyword clusters "with meaningful impressions" that the article covers poorly and new H2s. Ask for one H2 and its placement first, then the content. (3) Human-edit the output: update outdated facts, remove AI phrasing, give a direct answer up front. Add 2–3 sections. (4) Pick an underperforming page and ask Claude for the most natural internal-link spot and anchor in the updated page. (5) Update the intro and the published date. (6) URL Inspection > Request Indexing. About 1 hour per page. No numeric thresholds are given. — [Sturm, 2026-07-23](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/1-hour-seo-update-boost-best-worst-pages/)
- **S6 – Compact Keywords (low-competition bottom-of-funnel terms).** His course and article frame low-volume, high-intent keywords with low content and authority requirements (he cites about 415 average words per page to rank). GSC is used to find terms you already rank for. — [Sturm, "SEO – Down the Funnel"](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/seo-down-the-funnel/); [Pareto SEO](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/)
- **S7 – Quaid's "crawled – not indexed" trick.** Sturm says he used a trick from David Quaid to take a URL that had been "Crawled – not indexed" for 2 years to rank #1 within two weeks. It was discussed on The Edward Show (likely E846, PageRank/topical authority episode). The trick itself is not described in any snippet I could access. — [Sturm on X (snippet)](https://x.com/edwardeachday/status/1983173832056942994); [Apple Podcasts episode](https://podcasts.apple.com/lu/podcast/the-seo-playbook-that-actually-works-pagerank-topical/id1700357503?i=1000733862212&l=fr-FR)

### Inferences
- S2 and S3 together form a clean automatable rule: filter position 3–20, sort by impressions, and route each query to "expand existing page" or "new page + internal link from ranking page" based on relevance between query and page (an embedding or LLM judgement).
- Sturm's AI-filter prompts are UI-only. An API agent should reproduce them with regex (see the Mark Williams-Cook patterns in Q6).

### Gaps
- The exact Quaid trick (S7) is unconfirmed. It most likely concerns authority/PageRank (internal or external links), given Quaid's stated views (Q3), but this is inference.
- I found no Sturm material on programmatic-SEO-specific GSC workflows or Reddit SEO measured in GSC.

## Q3. David Quaid (Primary Position SEO, u/WebLinkr): views on GSC data

### Takeaway
Quaid's distinctive position is that "Crawled – currently not indexed" (and most indexing problems) is an **authority/PageRank problem, not a content-quality problem**. He says Google has no content-quality check at indexing time. He treats GSC as closer to the real "source of truth" than third-party rank trackers. Primary sources were mostly blocked (HTTP 403), so the evidence is thinner than for the others.

### Cited Findings
- **Q-1 – Crawled not indexed = authority, not quality.** On X he called the opposing tests "bad SEO testing". He says there is no content-quality check at indexing ("there's no time, there's no standard"), that "Crawled, Not Indexed" comes up on Reddit 5–7 times a day, and that he has never seen anyone fix it with "better quality". — [Quaid on X (snippet)](https://x.com/DavidGQuaid/status/2074904284341113019)
- **Q-2 – Internal links alone did not fix it at scale.** A search summary attributes to him a post saying that adding more and more internal links to 1.3K crawled-not-indexed URLs did nothing. — (snippet surfaced in search; primary URL not verifiable) — treat as unverified.
- **Q-3 – The Edward Show discussions.** Quaid and Sturm discuss why authority is often the missing piece when pages are crawled but not indexed. Sturm credits a Quaid trick with taking a 2-year crawled-not-indexed URL to #1 within 2 weeks (see S7). — [Sturm on X](https://x.com/edwardeachday/status/1983173832056942994); [The Edward Show](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-edward-show/id1700357503)
- **Q-4 – GSC vs rank trackers.** A podcast description says he argues GSC may become the real source of truth because rank trackers show positions that don't match what users see. His blog frames GSC as pre-click data and Analytics as post-click data. — (snippet) [Primary Position "GSC Performance"](https://primaryposition.com/blog/gsc-performance/); [Primary Position "How to Understand GSC KPIs and Performance Data"](https://primaryposition.com/blog/understand-gsc-performance-data/) (both returned 403 to fetch)
- **Q-5 – Relevance from document name and titles.** Primary Position summaries say he sees relevance as coming from the document name and in-page titles, and that position does most of the click work. — (snippet) [Primary Position](https://primaryposition.com/blog/understand-gsc-performance-data/)
- **Q-6 – Export before 16 months.** GSC deletes performance data after 16 months, so export it for YoY comparison. — (snippet) [Primary Position](https://primaryposition.com/blog/understand-gsc-performance-data/)
- He has a dedicated post, "Fix Google's GSC Indexing Status: Crawled, not Indexed [Resolved]", on primaryposition.com (title seen in search; content blocked). — [Primary Position author page](https://primaryposition.com/blog/author/david-quaid/page/4/)

### Inferences
- Agent rule derived from Quaid: when the Page Indexing report shows "Crawled – currently not indexed" for important URLs, do not default to "rewrite content". First check authority inputs: external links to the URL or folder, and internal links from pages that already get impressions or clicks in GSC. Then re-request indexing. This conflicts with Google, Ahrefs and Onely (see Q7).

### Gaps
- I could not access Quaid's Reddit comments (u/WebLinkr) or his blog body text. His exact stance on "Request Indexing" frequency, the meaning of impressions, and ranking-test methodology could not be verified from primary text.
- I found no YouTube transcript of his GSC content.

## Q4. Chris Long (Nectiv) and other "Chris" candidates

### Takeaway
Chris Long (co-founder of Nectiv, LinkedIn "chris-long-marketing", X @chris_nectiv) is the strongest match for "the LinkedIn guy named Chris". His signature GSC tactics are regex mining of conversational or "prompt-like" queries (10+ words), filters for `site:` fan-out queries, Claude-plus-GSC-export workflows, and commentary on AI Overview and AI Mode data in GSC. I found no GSC-tip content from Chris Green or Chris Reynolds. The only other "Chris" in GSC tip content was Chris Spann (Lumar podcast host).

### Cited Findings
- **CL1 – Mining conversational/LLM-style prompts.** Performance > Search results > Add filter > Query > Custom (regex) > `^(?:\S+\s+){9,}\S+$` (queries of 10+ words). Export and upload the results to Claude for theme and trend analysis. The article also tracked rising AI Mode impressions over about 3 months. — [Nectiv blog](https://nectivdigital.com/blog/how-to-mine-google-search-console-for-conversation-data-regex-included) (search snippet dates it **Feb 2026**); also covered in [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/see-ai-search-prompts-google-search-console-470358)
- **CL2 – `site:` operator queries as LLM fan-out footprints.** In a LinkedIn roundup he lists a filter for queries containing "site:". His team's data study found ChatGPT used `site:` in 65% of fan-out queries. — (snippet) [StartupTalky repost of Long's tip](https://community.startuptalky.com/discussions/post/seo-tip-there-s-been-a-ton-of-different-methods-on-how-to-mine-google-R4UDscuVZ6DSrWK)
- **CL3 – AI Overview data in GSC is incomplete.** He notes Google has not provided official AIO click metrics in Search Console, and that Google told Barry Schwartz it is "continuing to work with website owners". He also shared a study that 91% of AIOs appear in position 1. — (snippet) [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chris-long-marketing_holy-moly-an-updated-seo-study-from-ahrefs-activity-7425153713259855872-9N1m)
- **CL4 – Claude + GSC export → page prioritisation.** A Long post describes a Claude skill that takes a GSC export and scores each page by priority, content classification and expected effort. — (snippet, via search; exact post not fetched)
- **CL5 – "AI Content Audit Assistant" (Ian Lurie's method, amplified by Long, about 2025).** Claude Project with custom instructions, then a Zapier MCP to Google Drive/Sheets, then a Screaming Frog crawl with the GSC, GA and OpenAI-embedding APIs exported to Drive, then a 90-day GA4 comparison for traffic loss. Claude then finds pages with traffic drops, topic intent, and relevance via embeddings. Treat Claude "like a new employee" and expect re-prompting. — [Long LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chris-long-marketing_awesome-seo-article-how-to-build-an-ai-activity-7389287162841608192-dASS)
- **CL6 – Related AI-visibility tip (not GSC).** Find pages that influence LLM visibility but don't mention your brand, using an AI tracker plus Screaming Frog custom search with regex. — [X @chris_nectiv](https://x.com/chris_nectiv/status/1976632450357842380)
- Other Chris: the Lumar "Human-First Digital" podcast on Looker Studio and GSC tips is hosted by **Chris Spann** (guest Wojciech Urban). — [Lumar podcast](https://www.lumar.io/blog/podcast/looker-studio-google-search-console-tips-podcast-with-seo-wojciech-urban). No Chris Green or Chris Reynolds GSC material surfaced.

## Q5. Neil Patel

### Takeaway
Patel's GSC method is the classic content refresh: find pages with high impressions and low CTR or a page-1 average position, open their queries, and rewrite or retitle to target those queries. He reports +24–50% click gains in his own case studies.

### Cited Findings
- **NP1 – High-impressions/low-CTR refresh.** Performance > Pages, sort CTR ascending to find pages with many impressions but few clicks. Click the page, then Queries, to see which terms to target in the update. — (snippet) [neilpatel.com/br "Como ranquear seu conteúdo antigo"](https://neilpatel.com/br/blog/como-ranquear-conteudo/)
- **NP2 – Selection criteria from a Side Hustle Nation case study with Patel's method:** keywords already on page 1 (the snippet says "average position of 2 or lower"), abnormally low CTR, and high impressions. Result: +24.3% Google organic traffic to 8 posts, last 90 days vs the prior 90. — (snippet; the page blocked fetch) [Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/google-search-console-for-more-traffic/)
- **NP3 – Reported refresh outcome:** clicks up almost 50% and impressions up 102% in the quarter after a content refresh (own-site case study). — (snippet) [neilpatel.com "successful content marketing"](https://neilpatel.com/blog/successful-content-marketing/)
- He also has a GSC Crawl Stats guide — [neilpatel.com](https://neilpatel.com/blog/google-search-console-crawl-stats/) (not reviewed).

### Gaps
- I did not locate a dated Neil Patel YouTube video with a GSC workflow or timestamps. His blog posts are undated in the snippets.

## Q6. Other experts with GSC-specific methods

### Takeaway
The most concrete and automatable methods come from Glenn Gabe (before/after "delta reports" via the API), Mark Williams-Cook (intent regex library), Daniel Waisberg/Google (a 3-step performance analysis with annotations and the brand filter), Brodie Clark (AIO and new search-appearance filters and their caveats), and Kevin Indig (GSC data incompleteness). I found no GSC-specific methods sourced to Matt Diggity, Nathan Gotch, Julian Goldie, Sam Oh, Lily Ray or Aleyda Solis within budget.

### Cited Findings
- **Glenn Gabe – Delta report.** Trigger: core update, migration or redesign. Pull page (or query) × clicks/impressions/CTR/position for the period after the event vs the same-length period before, via the API (the UI caps exports at 1,000 rows). Sort by click change. Segment by page type with URL "contains" or regex (/category/, /products/, /blog/) and by search type and search appearance. Look for the biggest drops and surges to find "glaring issues". — [GSQi "Smart Delta Reports", 2021-04-12](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/how-to-automate-delta-report-gsc-api-analytics-edge/); he distinguishes relevancy adjustments, intent shifts and site-wide quality problems after broad core updates — [GSQi](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/google-broad-core-updates-difference-between-relevancy-adjustments-intent-shifts-overall-site-quality/). He also has a podcast episode saying sharp surges or drops in GSC indexing counts can be benign — [SEO From The Front Lines](https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/glenn-gabe/episodes/The-Scary-Surge-and-Drop-in-Indexing-in-Google-Search-Console-And-Why-That-Might-Be-Totally-Fine-e2l51ns)
- **Mark Williams-Cook – Intent regex library (2026-06-11).** Instead of "probably ok" AI classification, use regex on Query. Informational: `\b(how to|guide|tutorial|step by step|tips|tricks|ways to|best way to|learn|help|explain|understand|instruction|methods|examples|meaning of|definition)\b`. Comparison: `\b(best|vs|versus|compare|comparison|alternative|alternatives|better|cheaper|worse|cheapest|highest|lowest|top|difference|differences|differences between)\b`. Transactional: `\b(buy|purchase|price|cost|cheap|discount|deal|coupon|order|shop|store|near me|online|sale|best price|affordable|available|in stock)\b`. Navigational/reputation: `\b(review|reviews|rating|ratings|customer service|support|warranty|return policy|refund|complaint|feedback|scam|legit|trustworthy|experience|testimonial|problems|issues)\b`. SaaS tools (credited to Pietro Mingotti): `\b(?:tool|software|app|system|platform|application|program|solution|portal|suite|service)s?\b`. 5+ word queries: `([^ ]+\s){4,}`. — [MWC Substack note](https://substack.com/profile/299555284-mark-williams-cook/note/c-274334294) (the source text showed escaped backslashes, normalised here). He warns that applying any filter triggers "the great data redaction", meaning filtered views hide more anonymised queries. His example site had 148k total clicks, 39.7k branded and 30.8k non-branded, so most clicks were not attributed to any query. — (snippet) [Wix SEO Hub](https://www.wix.com/seo/learn/resource/google-search-console-for-keyword-research)
- **Daniel Waisberg (Google) – 3-step performance analysis (video ~Dec 2024).** (1) Set context: date range, search type (check Image, Video and News, not just Web), query include/exclude, Compare mode (previous period or YoY). (2) Read the chart's shape (weekday/weekend patterns, spikes), toggle all four metrics, and add **annotations** (right-click a date, up to 120 characters) for launches and fixes. Annotations are not shown in Compare mode or the 24-hour view. (3) Tables by query/page/country/device/appearance/date. Rules: expected queries missing → content gap; important pages missing → URL Inspection for technical issues; impressions far above clicks or very low CTR → consider images or structured data. Use the **Branded / Non-branded** filter (an AI classifier that includes misspellings). — [PPC Land summary](https://ppc.land/google-updates-search-console-performance-analysis-guidance/); the training series started Jan 2020 — [Google Search Central blog](https://developers.google.cn/search/blog/2020/01/search-console-training-videos?hl=en). Brand-filter accuracy caveat: it missed branded items and included competitor and executive names — [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/search-console-adds-brand-filters)
- **Brodie Clark – AIO measurement and new filters.** His controlled test (a rare query, US-only, logged-in users clicking an AIO citation) found AIO impressions and clicks are folded into normal web data. The US subset showed 13.5% CTR at average position 1.7. Google clarified (2024-08-15) that AIO impressions count only when the link is scrolled or expanded into view. He also notes that in several grid types an impression only registers after expansion, which can inflate CTR. — [brodieclark.com, updated 2026-06-08](https://brodieclark.com/ai-overviews-google-search-console/). He covered the "Translated results" search-appearance filter — [brodieclark.com](https://brodieclark.com/translated-results-filter-google-search-console/) — and a Web search type split into Text-based vs Multimodal, which has no query dimension (snippet).
- **Kevin Indig – Data incompleteness (Growth Memo, 2026-02-02).** An analysis of 450M impressions found Google filters about 3/4 of impressions (privacy) and about 38% of clicks. He also says bot inflation and AIOs "corrupt what remains". He prefers the API over the UI. — [Growth Memo (paywalled preview)](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/gsc-data-is-75-incomplete). Earlier work by Patrick Stox (Ahrefs) found almost 50% filtered — (snippet, cited in the same search). Indig on a podcast: impressions show what else Google has tried to rank your page for — (snippet) [Marketing Speak](https://www.marketingspeak.com/the-future-of-search-isnt-search-with-kevin-indig/)
- **Aleyda Solis.** She suggests a GSC query report filtered to brand terms as an own-site proxy for AI-driven influence ("influence happens without a click"). — (snippet) [Speaker Deck, 3-layer AI metrics framework](https://speakerdeck.com/aleyda/redefining-metrics-for-the-ai-search-era-a-3-layer-framework-to-measure-ai-presence-readiness-and-business-impact)

## Q7. Where experts disagree

### Takeaway
The biggest splits are about (1) what fixes "Crawled – not indexed" (authority vs quality) and whether resubmitting helps, (2) how much to trust impressions and average position, and (3) whether to use GSC's AI features (AI filter, brand classifier) or deterministic regex.

### Cited Findings
- **Request indexing:** Sturm requests indexing after every page edit and for key pages at setup — [Sturm 2026-07-23](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/1-hour-seo-update-boost-best-worst-pages/), [Pareto SEO](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/). Google's help says there is no need to resubmit "crawled – currently not indexed" URLs — [Google Page indexing help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203?hl=en). Ahrefs suggests requesting a recrawl after improving the page — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/crawled-currently-not-indexed/). Third-party tooling notes a limit of about 10 requests per day per account (unverified, tool description) — [Glama connector listing](https://glama.ai/mcp/connectors/dev.cotlet/cotlet/tools/google-search-console_request_indexing).
- **Crawled not indexed cause:** Quaid says authority, with no quality check at indexing — [X](https://x.com/DavidGQuaid/status/2074904284341113019). Ahrefs and Onely frame it as Google not considering the page valuable enough (quality/duplication/internal links) — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/crawled-currently-not-indexed/), [Onely](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-to-fix-crawled-currently-not-indexed-in-google-search-console/). Koray frames indexing and ranking through cost of retrieval and historical data (crawl efficiency plus topical consistency) — [SEO by Marta](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/).
- **Impressions as a signal:** Koray treats daily impressions as the leading KPI with a 10k/day re-ranking threshold — [SEO by Marta](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/). Indig sees impressions as Google "testing" the page for queries, but says about 75% are filtered and bots and AIOs distort the rest — [Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/gsc-data-is-75-incomplete). Brodie Clark: AIO and grid impressions only count when the link is scrolled or expanded into view, which skews CTR — [brodieclark.com](https://brodieclark.com/ai-overviews-google-search-console/).
- **Average position reliability:** Google defines it as the topmost position per query averaged across impressions (impression-weighted) — [Google help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828?hl=en). Sturm and Patel use it directly as a filter threshold (3–20; page 1) — [Sturm](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/). Quaid reportedly views GSC as more truthful than rank trackers — (snippet) [Primary Position](https://primaryposition.com/blog/gsc-performance/).
- **AI classification vs regex:** Sturm promotes GSC's AI filter prompts — [Sturm 2026-03-09](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-search-console-ai-seo-wins/). Mark Williams-Cook says to use deterministic regex instead of "probably ok" AI — [MWC note](https://substack.com/profile/299555284-mark-williams-cook/note/c-274334294). Google's brand filter is AI-based and has documented misclassifications — [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/search-console-adds-brand-filters).
- **UI vs API:** Gabe and Indig both prefer the API (no 1,000-row cap, less sampling) — [GSQi](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/how-to-automate-delta-report-gsc-api-analytics-edge/), [Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/gsc-data-is-75-incomplete).

### Inferences
- For an automation agent, a safe default is to use the API with regex segmentation, report anonymised-query share alongside any query-level conclusion, and request indexing only after a material page change (not as the fix for crawled-not-indexed). For crawled-not-indexed, run both an authority check (Quaid) and a duplication/quality check (Ahrefs/Google) before acting.

### Gaps
- Quaid's explicit position on request-indexing and on average position could not be confirmed from primary text.

## Q8. De-duplicated list of the most distinctive tactics (for the automation skill)

### Takeaway
Fifteen tactics cover nearly everything distinctive in this research. Each maps to GSC API calls (searchanalytics.query with dimensions and regex filters; urlInspection; sitemaps) or to the UI-only features noted.

### Cited Findings
1. **Position 3–20 "already ranking" router:** filter, sort by impressions, then expand the ranking page if the query is relevant, otherwise create a new page and link to it from the ranking page — [Sturm Pareto SEO](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/)
2. **Top-page query-cluster expansion:** export the page's queries, have an LLM find under-covered clusters, add 2–3 H2s, internally link to a weak page, refresh the date, request indexing — [Sturm 1-hour update](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/1-hour-seo-update-boost-best-worst-pages/)
3. **High-impressions/low-CTR page refresh:** pages sorted by CTR ascending, then retarget their queries — [Neil Patel (BR)](https://neilpatel.com/br/blog/como-ranquear-conteudo/)
4. **Prompt mining:** 10+ word queries with `^(?:\S+\s+){9,}\S+$`, then LLM theme clustering — [Nectiv](https://nectivdigital.com/blog/how-to-mine-google-search-console-for-conversation-data-regex-included)
5. **`site:` query filter** as an LLM fan-out footprint — [Long (snippet)](https://community.startuptalky.com/discussions/post/seo-tip-there-s-been-a-ton-of-different-methods-on-how-to-mine-google-R4UDscuVZ6DSrWK)
6. **Intent segmentation by regex library** (informational, comparison, transactional, navigational, SaaS tool, long-tail) — [Mark Williams-Cook](https://substack.com/profile/299555284-mark-williams-cook/note/c-274334294)
7. **Funnel/intent AI-filter prompts plus folder-based monitoring** (/services/, /blog/) — [Sturm](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-search-console-ai-seo-wins/)
8. **Delta report around dated events** (core update, migration), page and query level, segmented by page type and search appearance, via the API — [Glenn Gabe](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/how-to-automate-delta-report-gsc-api-analytics-edge/)
9. **Annotate events, then Compare mode, then table drill-down;** missing queries mean a content gap, missing pages mean URL Inspection — [Waisberg via PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-updates-search-console-performance-analysis-guidance/)
10. **Branded vs non-branded split and co-movement tracking** (brand demand as an authority KPI) — [Koray X](https://x.com/KorayGubur/status/1965191271330259231); [Waisberg brand filter](https://ppc.land/google-updates-search-console-performance-analysis-guidance/)
11. **Daily-impressions momentum milestone** (10k/day means re-ranking, so scale coverage) plus a 6-month refresh rule — [Koray](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
12. **Distinct-query-count growth per topical cluster** as the topical-map coverage KPI — [Holistic SEO case study](https://www.holisticseo.digital/seo-research-study/topical-map)
13. **Crawl Stats cost-of-retrieval KPIs** (99% 200/304, 99% canonical HTML hits, under 100 ms) — [Koray](https://seobymarta.com/blog/topical-authority-in-the-casino-industry-interview-koray-tugberk-gubur/)
14. **Crawled-not-indexed authority-first triage** (links and PageRank before content rewrites) — [Quaid X](https://x.com/DavidGQuaid/status/2074904284341113019)
15. **Data-quality guardrails:** report anonymised-query share, avoid conclusions from filtered views alone, export before 16 months, discount AIO and grid impressions — [Indig](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/gsc-data-is-75-incomplete), [MWC via Wix](https://www.wix.com/seo/learn/resource/google-search-console-for-keyword-research), [Brodie Clark](https://brodieclark.com/ai-overviews-google-search-console/)

### Inferences
- Items 1–3 overlap (all "page already has impressions, improve it"). The distinction is the trigger: position band (Sturm), CTR deficit (Patel), or under-covered query clusters on a top page (Sturm 1-hour). A single agent routine can compute all three per page and pick the strongest signal.
