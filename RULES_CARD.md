# Rules card (what the writer needs; RULES.md is the full reference, and `next` checks everything)

**The page**
1. Follow `write_brief.md` section 1 (page job): on a service, product, category, location or home page the client's offer and its next step come early and at the close; official and other sites are cited evidence, never the reader's destination; never write against the client. A guide answers fully; where the reader's task meets the client's offer, one short passage and its link.
2. Write every heading of the plan, in its order. Each answers its question in its first one or two sentences (the first paragraph under a question is 40–50 words, direct), then the detail. "also answer" lines are answered inside that section in those words. No FAQ block, no "Introduction", "Overview" or "Conclusion".
3. Facts: only the ones in the brief, in your own sentences (never six or more words in a row from any page), figures exactly as their notes give them, with the condition they carry (who, when, which product). No figure from memory. Never a losing figure from "Settled disagreements".
4. Links: the ones listed under each heading, on descriptive anchors of 2–6 words that name the target page, inside a sentence about it, never in the first sentence under a heading; link an authority on the words a reader would check; each page once; never a competitor.

**Sentences**
5. Address the reader as "you". Average 15–20 words with real variation; fewer than one sentence in ten under 8 words.
6. Name the thing: never open with a vague "This", "It" or "These" + verb. Name the actor of an opening "-ing" or "-ed" phrase.
7. No trailing commentary (", making it…", ", allowing you to…", ", ensuring…"), no teaser sentences ("Here's what to know."), no meta-commentary ("below", "this guide"), no colon reveals ("The catch: …"), no "not just/not only X but Y", no rule-of-three padding.
8. Banned words (and their forms): streamline, empower, delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, realm, vibrant, game-changer, cutting-edge, unparalleled, meticulous, leverage, foster, embark, elevate, unlock, seamless, effortless, robust, navigate, journey, deep dive, world-class, renowned, premier, tailored to, cater to, additionally, moreover, furthermore, "it's worth noting", "it's important to note", "plays a vital/crucial role", "in conclusion", "in today's…". Say the specific thing instead.
9. Headings in sentence case (capital on the first word and names only), under about 70 characters. Paragraphs of 2–4 sentences. Bold at most once per section, on the answer. At most one em dash per 150 words; no exclamation marks.
10. The locale's spelling, units and currency (en-GB: colour, metric, £). One name and one spelling per thing.
11. Money, health, legal and safety pages: the caution a professional must give, once, in the section where the decision is made, in concrete terms. No boilerplate disclaimers.

**Never**: mention the research ("top results", "other guides"), invent experience, statistics, credentials or customer stories, use HTML, or copy a competitor's wording.
