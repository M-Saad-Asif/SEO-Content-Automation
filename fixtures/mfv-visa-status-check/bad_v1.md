## Required Tracking Credentials: GWF, UAN, & Application Numbers

Checking an active visa application online requires specific primary credentials assigned during your application submission or biometric enrollment. Issuing authorities require specific reference formats alongside valid passport details. Required identifiers include Global Web Form references, Unique Application Numbers, consular barcode identifiers, or Visa Application Centre receipt codes.

Different immigration authorities issue distinct reference formats:

* **United Kingdom (UKVI):** Applicants use a Global Web Form (GWF) number or Unique Application Number (UAN) generated during digital submission. Accessing your digital eVisa requires setting up and linking an online UKVI account. Individual applicants confirm identity using the official UK Immigration: ID Check smartphone app with biometric passport chip scanning. Dependants, including partners and children joining a primary applicant, require their own individual UKVI accounts to access their respective eVisas. Electronic Travel Authorisations (ETAs) do not require an eVisa.
* **United States (CEAC):** Nonimmigrant visa applicants track progress using their DS confirmation barcode and consular interview location. Immigrant visa applicants use their permanent case number comprising their assigned embassy prefix and numeric identifier.
* **Schengen Area & Global VACs:** Commercial hubs require the reference number printed on your submission receipt. These codes combine mission location codes, filing year, and sequential filing indices paired with the applicant's surname.
* **Australia & Canada:** Australia uses an ImmiAccount Transaction Reference Number (TRN). Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) tracks files using an official Application Number paired with the applicant's Unique Client Identifier (UCI).

Always preserve your original submission confirmation emails, biometric appointment receipts, and stamped submission paperwork. If a reference number is misplaced, recovery requires reviewing initial submission confirmations or presenting your government passport directly at the processing center of submission.

## Understanding Your Visa Status: Under Review, Received, & Issued

Immigration authorities categorize application progress into standard operational milestones that indicate document custody, background verification, or final decision readiness. Typical stages include Application Received, Under Review, Administrative Processing, Decision Made, and Document Dispatched.

Decoding these status indicators correctly prevents premature inquiries and alerts travelers to necessary administrative actions:

* **Application Received:** The consular processing post or diplomatic mission has physically received your application dossier and biometric submission from the intake center. The file has been logged into the adjudicating database and awaits preliminary queue assignment.
* **Under Review / Being Processed:** An immigration caseworker or consular officer is actively evaluating your supporting evidence, assessing eligibility requirements, and reviewing financial, employment, and travel histories. In digital systems like the UK eVisa portal, this phase includes automated security and immigration record reconciliations.
* **Administrative Processing:** Mandatory secondary vetting, consular security consultations, or inter-agency reviews are underway. In consular tracking, administrative processing occurs before final visa issuance or formal refusal and can range from several business days to extended periods if additional background checks are triggered.
* **Additional Information Requested:** The caseworker requires updated bank statements, revised employer letters, or clarification regarding prior travel history. Responding within specified deadlines is critical to avoid statutory refusal on grounds of non-compliance.
* **Decision Made:** A formal legal outcome has been finalized by the diplomatic post. Consular regulations prohibit outsourced VAC staff from disclosing approval or refusal outcomes over telephone desks. The final decision remains sealed until communicated in writing or delivered with your returned passport.
* **Issued / Ready for Collection:** The physical visa vignette has been printed and adhered to your passport, or the digital record has been activated on the central immigration database. Applicants using digital permissions can view their verified status and generate secure temporary share codes through government verification portals to prove right to work, study, or rent.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Visa Status Tracking

Finding reliable answers to common visa tracking scenarios prevents unnecessary re-applications and clarifies decision timelines. Review direct answers to frequent applicant questions below.

**How often is official visa application tracking updated?**
Consular tracking databases and VAC custody records update once per business day, typically during evening hours after consular caseworkers log completed batches. Checking your status multiple times within the same day will not reveal new information.

**What should I do if my tracking status displays "No Records Found"?**
A "No Records Found" message usually indicates that intake staff have not yet scanned your submission into the central system. Initial database logging can require several business days following your biometric appointment. Alternatively, verify that you have entered your reference number without unintended spaces or typographical errors in your passport number.

**Can customer service staff at a Visa Application Centre disclose my visa decision?**
No. Commercial VAC partners operate solely as biometric and document intake facilities. Consular privacy regulations prohibit VAC staff from accessing or disclosing whether an application has been approved or rejected. Decisions remain sealed until delivered directly to the applicant.

**How do I share my UK eVisa status with an employer or landlord?**
Log into the official GOV.UK View and Prove service using your UKVI account credentials. Select the reason you need to share your immigration status to generate a temporary share code. Provide this code alongside your date of birth to your employer, educational institution, or landlord to verify your lawful status securely online.

**Does MasterFastVisas guarantee visa issuance?**
No agency or commercial intermediary can guarantee sovereign visa issuance. Visa grants remain under the exclusive statutory authority of national immigration departments.
