# Where Is My Visa Application?

A visa status check works only through the government deciding your application. Choose that country below to reach its official route and the details to have ready. Some governments let you check your visa application status online; others write to you when they decide.

## How do you check a UK visa application status?

You wait for UKVI to contact you. UKVI has no public online application tracker. UKVI sends an email or letter when it has made a decision, and also if your application will take longer than usual. Check your junk folder as well as your inbox.

GOV.UK publishes the current processing time for each visa type. If you paid for the priority or super priority service, use the time published for that service.

Before that time has passed, the UKVI contact centre can't help. Staff give an update only when both of these apply:

1. UKVI has taken longer than usual to process your application.
2. UKVI has not already contacted you about your application.

Even then, [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/contact-ukvi-inside-outside-uk/y/outside-the-uk/checking-when-you-will-get-your-visa-decision) is plain about it: "UKVI contact centre staff cannot tell you when you will get your visa decision".

## When is a paid UKVI query worth it?

Only once your application has passed its usual processing time and UKVI hasn't emailed or written to you. From outside the UK, every query costs money, so an early one buys nothing. An email query is usually the better choice.

I'd send one email query and skip the phone line. The email costs £2.74 and follow-ups about it are free, while a call runs at 69 pence a minute for an update that has to meet the same two conditions.

| Route | Cost from outside the UK | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| [Email query](https://www.gov.uk/contact-ukvi-inside-outside-uk/y/outside-the-uk/checking-when-you-will-get-your-visa-decision) | £2.74; follow-up emails about the same query are free | A reply within 5 days |
| Phone call to the contact centre | 69 pence per minute on top of your network charges | An update if both conditions apply |

## Where do you find your eVisa after a UK grant?

In your UKVI account. You need the account to access an eVisa, and the eVisa only appears once you link it to the account.

To [set up a UKVI account](https://www.gov.uk/evisa/set-up-ukvi-account), you can use a valid passport and your visa application number. That number is your GWF or UAN number.

## How does a US visa status check work?

You check online on the State Department's [CEAC Visa Status Check](https://ceac.state.gov/CEACStatTracker/Status.aspx). Enter your CEAC barcode or case number and the location where your interview took place. The barcode is the application ID from your DS-160 application.

If the consulate asked you for more information, wait at least three business days after sending it before you check again. The [US Visa Status Check](https://masterfastvisas.com/visa-status-check/usa) page covers the details for each step.

## What does "Refused" under 221(g) mean?

Administrative processing counts as a visa denial under Section 221(g), so CEAC shows "REFUSED" while your case is still being processed. The status changes to "ISSUED" only after the embassy receives your passport and prints your visa.

The consular officer tells you at the end of the interview if your case needs administrative processing. The [US Embassy in Oman's guide to administrative processing](https://om.usembassy.gov/administrative-processing-nonimmigrant-visas/) explains the steps that follow.

## What if you need to travel before a decision?

Check the published processing time first, then use the official route on your country's page. A query sent before that time has passed rarely gets you a date.
