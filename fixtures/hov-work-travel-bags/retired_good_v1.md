# Bags for work and travel in the UK

A bag that works for the office and for a flight has to pass one test that product photos don't show: the airline's sizer. This guide checks five bags against the cabin limits that easyJet, Ryanair, Wizz Air and British Airways publish. The fit results come from each bag's listed dimensions, so a bag packed past its seams can measure larger.

## Which bag fits which airline allowance?

The Eagle Canary Laptop Bag and the Peter James Marloes Holdall both fit easyJet's free under-seat size. Only the Canary also fits Ryanair's Priority bag with room to spare. The Fantana Shoreditch Holdall needs a full cabin-bag allowance, such as British Airways'.

The airlines measure differently. easyJet's 45 × 36 × 20 cm under-seat limit includes handles and wheels, and so does British Airways' 56 × 45 × 25 cm cabin bag. Wizz Air's 40 × 30 × 20 cm free bag does not count handles and wheels.

## Which laptop bag fits under the seat on easyJet?

The Eagle Canary Laptop Bag measures 43 × 31 × 15 cm, which leaves 2 cm spare on its longest side against easyJet's free under-seat limit. It takes laptops up to 15.6" in a padded compartment and weighs 0.9 kg.

For a work week, the Canary does the office part well. The top handle suits meetings, and you can attach the shoulder strap for the walk to the station. A rear strap slides over the handle of a wheeled case, so the laptop bag rides on your suitcase through the airport.

Its limit is the strictest free allowances. The Canary is 43 cm long, 3 cm over the 40 cm length that Ryanair and Wizz Air allow for a free small bag. British Airways' handbag or backpack size is also 40 × 30 × 15 cm, so on BA the Canary needs to travel as your cabin bag, not your personal item. The Canary comes in black and in [coffee brown](https://houseofvelorien.com/products/eagle-canary-laptop-bag-coffee-brown).

## Which holdall fits easyJet's free under-seat size?

The Peter James Marloes Holdall, at 45 × 33 × 20 cm, fits easyJet's free under-seat limit of 45 × 36 × 20 cm. The holdall has no room at all on the longest or shortest side, so it passes only when packed to its listed shape.

That zero margin is the thing to plan around. A full holdall bulges, and easyJet counts handles in its measurement. If you fill the Marloes to the zip, expect it to fail the sizer.

The same bag is too big for Ryanair's and Wizz Air's free small bags, which allow 40 cm in length. It fits Ryanair's Priority 10kg bag with no spare room on the shortest side, and it fits British Airways' cabin bag with 11 cm spare on the longest side. The outer is pebbled faux leather, in brown, teal pink, beige or black. Find it on the [Peter James Marloes Holdall](https://houseofvelorien.com/products/peter-james-marloes-holdall) page.

## Will the Fantana Shoreditch Holdall fit Ryanair's Priority bag size?

No. The [Fantana Shoreditch Holdall](https://houseofvelorien.com/products/fantana-shoreditch-holdall) is 22 cm at its shortest side, and Ryanair's Priority bag allows 20 cm. It does fit the British Airways cabin bag size and the WIZZ Priority trolley bag size.

The WIZZ Priority fit is tight, with 1 cm spare on both the longest and shortest sides. On British Airways, the margin is 2 cm on the longest side.

The Shoreditch Holdall is the longest bag here, at 54 cm. It suits a few nights away, with a main compartment and outer pockets for clothes, shoes and smaller items. It weighs 0.9 kg and comes in olive, navy or black. If your airline gives you only a free under-seat bag, choose one of the smaller bags instead.

## When does a shoulder bag or messenger make more sense?

A shoulder bag suits days when you carry less than a laptop bag holds. The [Fantana Shoreditch Shoulder Bag](https://houseofvelorien.com/products/fantana-shoreditch-shoulder-bag) weighs 0.5 kg and has a wide top opening. Its listed shape widens from a 30 cm base to 50 cm at the top.

Measured at its widest point, the shoulder bag is too big for every free under-seat size in this guide: easyJet's, Ryanair's, Wizz Air's and British Airways' handbag size. Unexpanded, it fits the British Airways cabin bag and Ryanair's Priority bag, with no spare room on the shortest side for Ryanair. The bag expands, and the fit results assume it is not expanded.

The [Peter James Ennismore Messenger](https://houseofvelorien.com/products/peter-james-ennismore-messenger) is the smallest bag in the range. The messenger has a zip-top main compartment and a U-zip front pocket, in a textured faux cross-grain finish. Its listed dimensions look incomplete, so this guide makes no airline fit claim for it. Measure it against your airline's limit before you rely on it as a free bag.

## How do you check a bag before you fly?

Measure the bag packed, with handles and wheels included. Then compare it with the allowance on your booking, not a general rule. The allowances in this guide come from the airlines' own pages:

- [easyJet cabin bag rules](https://www.easyjet.com/en/help/baggage/cabin-bag-and-hold-luggage)
- [Ryanair bag policy](https://help.ryanair.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12888036565521-Ryanair-s-Bag-Policy)
- [Wizz Air cabin baggage](https://www.wizzair.com/en-gb/help-centre/booking-information-and-services/baggage/baggage-allowance/cabin-baggage)
- [British Airways baggage essentials](https://www.britishairways.com/content/information/baggage-essentials)

Airlines change these sizes, so check the page again close to your flight. House of Velorien sells bags from Eagle, Fantana and Peter James and ships across the UK.
