## How do you care for vegan PU leather and travel nylon bags?

To care for vegan PU leather, wipe the surface with a soft, damp microfiber cloth and mild soapy water while avoiding alcohol-based solvents, waxes, and direct heat; for travel nylon, remove surface dust with a moist sponge, brush seam grit with a soft bristle brush, and allow the fabric to air-dry naturally away from radiators.

The British climate demands bags that endure damp train platforms, sudden rain showers, and muddy street spray without degrading. Both structured vegan polyurethane (PU) leather and high-density travel nylon offer practical weather resistance, yet each material requires specific maintenance routines to extend its working lifespan:

**Vegan PU Leather Care Guidelines**  
Structured PU leather (used in the [Eagle Office Laptop Bag](https://houseofvelorien.com/collections/bags), [Peter James Holdall](https://houseofvelorien.com/collections/bags), and [Peter James Messenger Bag](https://houseofvelorien.com/collections/bags)) features a solid polyurethane surface layer that stops water from soaking into the core fabric.
- **Regular Cleaning**: Wipe exterior panels once every fortnight using a microfiber cloth dampened with lukewarm water and a drop of gentle dishwashing soap. Dry thoroughly with a clean towel.
- **Avoid Leather Conditioners**: Unlike animal hides, synthetic PU leather has no porous biological grain to absorb oils. Applying traditional leather balms, dubbin, or saddle soap leaves a sticky residue that attracts platform dust and damages the synthetic coat.
- **Avoid Solvent Hand Sanitizers**: High-concentration alcohol wipes and hand sanitizers dissolve the polyurethane topcoat, resulting in cloudy discoloration or surface peeling. Keep sanitizer bottles inside sealed pouches rather than loose in outer pockets.

**High-Density Travel Nylon Care Guidelines**  
Tightly woven synthetic nylon (used in the [Fantana Voyager Shoulder Bag](https://houseofvelorien.com/collections/bags) and [Fantana Voyager Holdall](https://houseofvelorien.com/collections/bags)) excels at tear resistance and weight reduction.
- **Spot Washing**: For coffee drips or street splashes, blot the stain immediately with an absorbent cloth. Sponge the area with warm water and neutral detergent, working from the outside of the mark inward to avoid spreading rings.
- **Hardware Maintenance**: Train grit can accumulate in coil zips during daily use. Brush the teeth with a dry toothbrush once a month to ensure smooth gliding. Applying a light stroke of beeswax to metal teeth protects against sticking in cold weather.
- **Drying and Storage**: If caught in heavy rain, empty your bag completely, pull out internal lining pockets, and air-dry at room temperature. Never place nylon or PU bags directly against hot radiators or inside tumble dryers, as intense localized heat weakens synthetic seams and adhesive bonds.

**Reversal Condition**: While water-resistant PU leather and treated nylon repel standard UK rain showers, they are not submersible sealed containers. If your commute involves extensive cycling in torrential rain or marine exposure, protect sensitive electronics inside a dedicated dry pouch or fit an external weather-resistant rain cover over your carry.
